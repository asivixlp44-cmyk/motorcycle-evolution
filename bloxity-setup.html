Description
Hop on your motorcycle and ride! Every step gives you +Speed, Speed levels you up, and every level makes your bike faster. Race through 6 stages of a neon city: jump across floating blocks, leap over barriers and pop-up spikes, stop for cars bursting out of tunnels, dodge highway traffic and outrun the police in the giant police square. Reach the end of each stage for Wins, unlock 16 motorcycles up to the Celestial God (+25K/Speed), train on treadmills up to 100x, rebirth for bigger boosts and race your friends to the top of the leaderboards.

After you create the game

  1. Legion has no custom env vars, so nothing is set on hosting.bloxity.io. On Legion the server runs in Bux mode by itself (purchases cost real Bux) and signs progress backups with the JWT_SECRET Legion injects.
  2. The webhook is protected by a secret key in its URL: set the IAP webhook URL to https://motorcycle-evolution.host.bloxity.io/api/legion-webhook?key=<webhook key>. The key is not in this repo (only its SHA-256, in server/src/bloxity.js); a webhook without it is rejected.
  3. Buy one cheap item to test.
